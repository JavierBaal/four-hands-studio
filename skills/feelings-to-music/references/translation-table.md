# Translation table: sensations to musical levers

Starting points, not recipes. Combine levers: they add up. Spanish words first because users often speak Spanish; English equivalents in brackets.

## 1. Emotions and moods

| Sensation | Mode / harmony | Register | Density | Tempo / pulse | Dynamics | Articulation / timbre | Space |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Triste, melancólico** (sad, melancholic) | Minor, Aeolian; suspensions that resolve late; descending lines | Middle-low | Few voices | Slow, rubato | Soft, swelling phrases | Legato, strings, piano with pedal | Some reverb |
| **Nostalgia** (nostalgia) | Major with borrowed minor chords (iv, bVI); added 9ths | Middle | Sparse | Slow to moderate | Soft | Warm, slightly blurred (pedal, long tails) | Wet |
| **Esperanza** (hope) | Major or Lydian; rising bass; unresolved IV | Rising from low to high | Grows | Moderate, steady | Crescendo with arrival | Legato, brightening | Opens up |
| **Íntimo** (intimate) | Simple, close voicings | Middle | 1–3 voices | Free, slow | Very soft | Close piano, solo string | Dry, near |
| **Calma, paz** (calm, peace) | Consonant, pedal tones, slow harmonic rhythm | Middle-high | Sparse | Slow, regular | Soft | Long notes, soft attacks | Wide reverb |
| **Alegre** (happy) | Major | Middle-high | Moderate | Faster | Moderate | Staccato, bright | — |
| **Tensión** (tension) | Pedal with dissonance above, semitone clashes, unresolved dominants | Extremes (very low + very high) | Grows | Ostinato, accelerating | Crescendo | Tremolo, repeated notes | — |
| **Miedo, amenaza** (fear, threat) | Mode matters little: clusters, tritones, chromatic lines | Low | Moves in and out | Irregular or very slow | Loud swells, sudden drops | Low brass, low strings, noise | Dark, close |
| **Inquietante, enfermizo** (unsettling, sick) | Minimal harmony: drones, minor 2nds, Phrygian/Locrian colour | Mixed: naive high + menacing low | Mechanical layers | Limping or shifting meter, polymeter | Uneven accents | Childlike timbres (celesta, music box) against noise; breaths | Dry |
| **Claustrofóbico** (claustrophobic) | Narrow clusters, chromatic circling | Narrow band | Dense in a small range | Insistent pulse | Constant, then pressing | Close voicings, muted sounds | Dry, no air at the top |
| **Misterio** (mystery) | Modal (Dorian, Lydian), parallel chords, no cadences | Middle-high | Sparse | Slow, suspended | Soft | Harp, celesta, harmonics, soft pads | Wide |
| **Épico, heroico** (epic) | Minor with bVI–bVII–i, power fifths, modal | Full range | Builds to full | Strong, steady, often 4/4 ostinato | Big arc | Brass, choir, low percussion | Large hall |
| **Soledad, vacío** (loneliness, emptiness) | Open fifths, no third, sparse | Wide gaps between voices | Very sparse | Slow, silences | Soft | Solo line, long tails | Very wet or very dry |
| **Frío, distante** (cold, distant) | Quartal or open harmony, no thirds | High | Sparse | Regular | Soft, even | Glass, bells, high strings senza vibrato | Far |
| **Cálido** (warm) | Thirds and sixths, major, smooth voice leading | Middle-low | Moderate | Moderate | Soft-moderate | Strings, horns, round piano | Near |
| **Agresivo** (aggressive) | Power chords, chromatic riffs | Low-middle | Dense | Fast, driving | Loud, accented | Distorted synths, low brass, hard attacks | Dry |
| **Onírico** (dreamlike) | Unrelated chords by common tone, modal shifts, slow glides | High | Blurred | Floating, no strong beat | Soft | Pads, harp, reversed-feel swells | Very wet |
| **Mecánico** (mechanical) | Static harmony | Any | Layered loops | Strict grid, repeating cells | Even | Short, percussive | Dry |
| **Orgánico, humano** (organic, human) | Any | — | Varying | Played deviations, rubato | Uneven, phrase-shaped | Acoustic instruments | Natural room |
| **Infantil, ingenuo** (childlike) | Major, simple, stepwise | High | Sparse | Moderate | Soft | Celesta, glockenspiel, music box, pizzicato | — |
| **Triunfal** (triumphant) | Major, strong cadences, fanfare intervals | Full | Full | Steady | Loud arrival | Brass, timpani, full strings | Large |

## 2. Images and textures

| Image | Possible translation |
|---|---|
| Una ciudad de noche, neón | Analog synth pulses, minor or Dorian, sub bass, arpeggios, wet reverb |
| Algo que se pudre / se infecta | A clean motif that degrades: notes dropped, displaced, detuned by a semitone, layers of noise taking over |
| Algo que se acerca / invasión | A foreign note entering softly and growing (semitone above the tonic), crescendo over the whole piece, unresolved ending |
| Respirar, aliento | Breath or noise sounds as rhythm; phrases that start and stop like breathing |
| Máquina | Repeating cells, polymeter that phases, strict grid, metallic short sounds |
| Agua, flujo | Arpeggios, legato, harp and piano, overlapping waves of dynamics |
| Espacio, inmensidad | Very low pedal + very high air, empty middle, slow changes |
| Recuerdo, memoria | A motif returning changed, softer, with blurred harmony |

## 3. Timbre adjectives

| Word | Usually means | Levers |
|---|---|---|
| Brillante (bright) | More high frequencies, attack | Higher register, brighter instruments, harder velocities |
| Oscuro (dark) | Fewer highs | Lower register, minor colour, muted or soft sounds |
| Cálido (warm) | Low-mid richness, soft attack | Middle-low register, thirds, legato |
| Áspero, sucio (rough, dirty) | Noise, distortion, beating | Close intervals, distorted synths, irregular timing |
| Redondo (round) | Soft attack, full | Soft velocities, sustained sounds, octave doublings |
| Duro, seco (hard, dry) | Sharp attack, little reverb | Short notes, accents, dry sounds |
| Etéreo (ethereal) | High, soft, wide | High register, pads, harmonics, wet |
| Pesado (heavy) | Low, dense, slow | Low octave doublings, slow pulse, loud |

## 4. Relative adjustments

| They say | Try first | Then, if not enough |
|---|---|---|
| Más aire / que respire | Remove a sustained layer; add rests in the inner parts | Open the voicings; add a sparse high layer |
| Menos denso / más limpio | Remove doublings | Thin rhythmic layers; move one part out of the crowded octave |
| Más profundidad | Add a low foundation and a high air layer | Add a contrasting emotional line (a slow melody against the machinery); vary repeats |
| Más oscuro | Lower register | Minor or Phrygian colour; darker sounds |
| Más luminoso | Higher register | Major or Lydian colour; brighter sounds |
| Más tensión | Add a dissonant pedal or ostinato | Accelerate harmonic rhythm or pulse; crescendo |
| Más sucio | Close intervals in the middle, uneven timing | Noise sounds; mark distorted synths for later |
| Más lejos | Softer velocities, longer notes | Reverb send (when available), higher register |
| Más cerca | Fewer voices, drier, middle register | Solo instrument, played deviations |
| Es brusco | Stagger entrances and exits; leave tails | Add a transition bar; shape velocities into the change |
| Suena programado | Vary velocities around the line; non-uniform deviations | Vary repeats; shape crescendos as phrases |
| Le falta algo | Ask by contrast: ¿falta peso abajo, brillo arriba, o una melodía que cante? | — |
